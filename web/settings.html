Turn on smart detection?

With it on, CraftPulse shows exactly what you're doing in each Craft app (the tool, dialog and document or photo) instead of a generic “Editing”. Your Craft apps need to be restarted once.

What changes: the apps open a private connection on your computer that CraftPulse reads. It stays on this machine, but other programs on it could also use it, so skip this if you share the computer with software you don't trust.
